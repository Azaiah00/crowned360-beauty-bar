Loc questions

Asked & answered.

Do I need a consultation before microlocs or loc extensions?

Yes. A consultation is required for all new loc extensions, microlocs and micro twists. Book it on StyleSeat (from $35, about 30 minutes).

How much is the deposit for microlocs?

The Micro Locs and Loc Extension Deposit Fee is $350, paid on StyleSeat to hold your install date.

How long does a microloc install take?

StyleSeat lists Micro Locs Installation and MicroLoc Extensions at about 8 hours, so plan a full day.

How much is a microloc retightening?

Microloc Retightening starts at $225 (about 3 hours 15 minutes). Partial MicroLoc Retightening starts at $175.

Can you repair or reattach broken locs?

Yes. Loc Reattachment is $15 per loc, a Partial Installation is from $575, and Loc Reconstruction is from $575. Wick Tightening/Repair starts at $165.

Do you start locs for kids?

Yes. Kids Starter Locs (8 years and younger) start at $100, and a Kids Retwist with Style (under 8) starts at $100.
