Before you book

Fees & policies.

Straight from Lakeisha’s StyleSeat profile, so there are no surprises on the day.

Deposit

“Lakeisha Mells requires a deposit to be paid at time of booking in order to secure an appointment. This deposit is non-refundable, and will be kept if you cancel without rescheduling.”

Late cancellation & no-show

“If you cancel less than 24 hours prior or fail to show up at your appointment, your deposit will be kept and you will be charged the difference between your deposit and 25% of service price for cancelling with less than 24 hours notice and 50% if you fail to show up at your appointment.”

Are the prices on this page final?

They are starting prices as listed on StyleSeat. Length, density, hair added and style can change the final price, which is confirmed at booking.

Do some services need a consultation?

Yes. A consultation (from $35) is required for all new loc extensions, microlocs and micro twists, and some other services may need one too.

Can I book after 5 PM or on a weekend?

Yes, by request. After-hours appointments (after 5 PM, Saturday or Sunday) carry a $35 fee. Call (762) 245-1983 to arrange one.
