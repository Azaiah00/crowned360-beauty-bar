Questions

How do I book an appointment at Crowned360 Beauty Bar?

Book online on StyleSeat at styleseat.com/m/v/crowned360. You choose the service and time, and pay a deposit to secure the appointment. Questions? Call or text (762) 245-1983.

Is there a deposit?

Yes. A non-refundable deposit is paid when you book. It is kept if you cancel without rescheduling. Microloc and loc extension bookings use a $350 deposit fee.

What is the cancellation and no-show policy?

Cancel or reschedule at least 24 hours ahead. With less than 24 hours notice, the deposit is kept and you are charged up to 25% of the service price; for a no-show, up to 50%.

What are your hours?

Monday to Friday, 8 AM to 5 PM. Appointments after 5 PM, on Saturday or on Sunday are available by request with a $35 after-hours fee; call (762) 245-1983.

How much is a loc retwist?

A Loc Re-twist (Only) starts at $85 for neck length and $95 for mid back on StyleSeat. Retwists with braids, plaits, curls or an updo start at $135 to $165.

How much do microlocs cost in Augusta at Crowned360?

Micro Locs Installation starts at $975 and MicroLoc Extensions with hair included start at $2,400. A consultation and a $350 deposit are required first.

Where are you located?

3128 Deans Bridge Rd, Ste D, Augusta, GA 30906, on Deans Bridge Road (US-1) in South Augusta, close to I-520 Exit 5.

Is there a late fee?

Yes, StyleSeat lists a $25 late fee. A $100 holiday fee applies on New Year’s, July 4th, Thanksgiving, Memorial Day, Christmas Eve and Christmas.
